About HopeBridge

Connecting people who want to help with orphanages that need support.

HopeBridge is a platform where verified orphanages can share their essential supply needs and birthday meal requirements. Donors can explore approved requests, show their interest and help fulfil those needs. Our goal is to make support simple, transparent and meaningful.

How It Works

A simple process that connects verified needs with willing donors.

1

Orphanages Register

Orphanages create an account and submit their details for verification.

2

Requests Get Approved

Admins review orphanages and their supply or birthday meal requests.

3

Donors Help

Donors choose an approved request and coordinate fulfilment after admin approval.
